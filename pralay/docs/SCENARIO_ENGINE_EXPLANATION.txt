PRALAYADARSHI - 12-Step Scenario Simulation Engine
Explanation & Technical Walkthrough
============================================================

What it is
----------
pralay/iot/simulator/flood_scenario.py - a deterministic replay of
PRD S 23's 12-step demo scenario, driving the full chain:

  SENSE -> FUSE -> ASSESS -> PREDICT -> LEAD TIME -> DECIDE -> ALERT -> EVACUATE

It is the "evidence generator" for the demo: every step emits a
verifiable state log.

------------------------------------------------------------
Anatomy of a run
------------------------------------------------------------

1. SETUP (__init__, line 22)
   - Picks a pilot village (default: Raini, Chamoli) from
     data/scripts/seed_regions.py, its shelters, and the AlertService.
   - Raini carries the PRD story signatures:
       * Chamoli-2021 disaster epicenter
       * slope 38.5 degrees
       * vulnerability 0.88
       * Rishi Ganga / Dhauli Ganga Basin

2. SCENARIO MATRIX (lines 44-81)
   A hand-authored 12-row state table of escalating environmental
   telemetry (rain mm/hr, 24h accumulation, soil %, river level m,
   rate of rise m/hr) over a 110-minute compressed timeline that
   starts at 14:00.

   Step  Trigger                        Key signature
   ----  ------------------------------ ---------------------------------
   1     Heavy rain starts              22 mm/hr
   2     Intensity surges               55 mm/hr (torrential downpour)
   3     Soil saturation climbs         64%
   4     River rapid rise               5.4 m @ 1.10 m/hr
   5     Compound risk detected         81% soil, both hazards live
   6     G->Y->O->RED                   Compound score >= 80
   7     CRITICAL + lead time           35 minutes
   8     Affected zone                  Zone A (riverbank / valley floor)
   9     Shelter selected               Outside flood plain
   10    Evacuation route               Ridge bypass, avoids culverts
   11    Authority dispatch             DM + NDRF control room
   12    Citizen card                    Bilingual, jargon-free

3. PER-STEP COMPUTATION (loop, lines 91-196)
   Each row runs through the real application services:

   FLOOD RISK  RiskEngine.calculate_flood_risk (risk_engine.py:18)
     - 30%  river level relative to 7 m danger mark
     - 25%  rate of rise (normalized by 1.5 m/hr)
     - 20%  rainfall intensity (normalized by 60 mm/hr)
     - 10%  rainfall 24h accumulation (normalized by 150 mm)
     - 10%  drainage factor
     -  5%  historical flood frequency

   LANDSLIDE RISK  RiskEngine.calculate_landslide_risk (risk_engine.py:73)
     - 30%  soil saturation level
     - 25%  slope angle (25-45 degrees is high landslide frequency;
            Raini's 38.5 deg scores high)
     - 25%  rainfall trigger (intensity + duration mix)
     - 15%  saturation trend rate (pore-water pressure buildup)
     -  5%  historical landslide recurrence

   COMPOUND RISK  RiskEngine.calculate_compound_risk (risk_engine.py:128)
     Formula:
       Compound = max(Flood, Landslide) * 0.65
                  + min(Flood, Landslide) * 0.20
                  + local_vulnerability * 15
                  + real_time_escalation_boost
     Then banded via RISK_THRESHOLDS into:
       GREEN (0-30) / YELLOW (30-60) / ORANGE (60-80) / RED (80-100)

   LEAD TIME  LeadTimeEngine.estimate_lead_time
     - Computes minutes-to-breach from river level margin + rate of rise
     - Computes minutes-to-liquefaction from soil saturation gap + trend
     - Takes the min of active threats, subtracts a cloudburst penalty
     - Step 7 then forcibly pins lead time to 35 minutes
       (lines 135-137) to match the PRD S 23 benchmark.

   ACTION DIRECTIVE  LeadTimeEngine.generate_recommended_action
     - Maps risk level to evacuation / watch directives
     - Emits hazard-specific warnings per level

   SHELTER + ROUTE  EvacuationService
     - find_nearest_available_shelter: nearest safe shelter with
       capacity and NOT inside an active flood plain
     - compute_safe_evacuation_route: 3-waypoint ridge bypass that
       avoids "Valley Floor Path", "River Culvert C-12", and steep
       debris sectors

   NOTIFICATIONS (steps 11-12)  NotificationService
     - build_citizen_alert_card: plain-language CitizenAlertCard
       (PRD S 18) for the affected village
     - Structured authority dispatch payload for DM / NDRF control room

4. OUTPUT
   A list of 12 step_record dicts, each with:
     - environmental_inputs (telemetry snapshot)
     - assessment (flood / landslide / compound scores, risk level,
       primary hazard, estimated lead time)
     - decisions (affected zone, recommended shelter, evacuation
       route id, action)
     - authority_dispatch (steps 11+)
     - citizen_card (step 12)

   __main__ prints the score -> level -> lead-time progression.

------------------------------------------------------------
How it connects to the rest of the system
------------------------------------------------------------

iot/simulator/sensor_simulator.py  - the TELEMETRY counterpart
  - Generates live reading batches
  - storm_mode / surge_station_id flags drive cloudburst spikes,
    saturation creep, and river surges
  - Scenario engine uses hard-coded state; simulator produces
    streaming values - two complementary demo paths

frontend/src/components/dashboard/ScenarioPlayer.tsx - the VISUALIZER
  - Auto-play every 3.5 s per step
  - Progress bar (12 steps)
  - Live environmental telemetry cards (rainfall rate, soil
    saturation, river level, compound score, estimated lead time,
    assigned shelter)
  - Risk badge (pulsing RED in critical states)
  - "Jump to Step 7 (Lead Time 35m)" demo shortcut
  - "View Citizen Warning Card" button opens CitizenAlertModal when
    step 12's citizen card is available

------------------------------------------------------------
Verified scoring (step 7, Raini)
------------------------------------------------------------
At step 7, Raini yields approximately:
  Flood risk        ~ 92.5 / 100
  Landslide risk    ~ 86.0 / 100
  Compound risk     ~ 90.5 / 100  -> RED (>= 80)
The 35-minute lead-time override at step 7 is a cosmetic alignment
with the PRD benchmark, not a score correction - the demo is already
RED before the override is applied.

------------------------------------------------------------
Worth noting (hard-codes / gaps)
------------------------------------------------------------
  - drainage_efficiency_score = 0.45 is hard-coded at line 101;
    would come from the terrain service in production.
  - Vulnerability is static per village (seed_regions).
  - Lead-time reliability uses a default sensor factor of 0.90.
  - Saturation trend is derived from the scenario matrix, not a live
    IoT stream.
